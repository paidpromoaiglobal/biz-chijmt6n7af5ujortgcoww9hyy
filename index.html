About Us

TK Multi Rehabilitation Centre (TKMRC)

TK Multi Rehabilitation Centre (TKMRC) is a trusted multi-specialty rehabilitation facility located in Maraimalai Nagar, Tamil Nadu. With a perfect 5-star rating backed by 72 satisfied patients, TKMRC is dedicated to delivering compassionate, evidence-based therapeutic care to individuals recovering from injuries, surgeries, neurological conditions, and mobility challenges. Our expert team combines modern rehabilitation techniques with personalized treatment plans to ensure every patient achieves the best possible outcome.

Conveniently situated at Anna Salai Road, NH-1, Maraimalai Nagar, our centre is easily accessible to residents across the region. We offer six days of extended hours and Sunday consultations to accommodate busy schedules and urgent care needs. Whether you are seeking physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, or specialized rehabilitation support, TKMRC is your partner in healing — guiding you every step of the way toward a healthier, more active life.
